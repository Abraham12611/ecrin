# 01 — Creative vision and art direction

## The idea

**ÉCRIN** reinterprets a media player as a precious personal object. The restraint of a luxury accessory meets the responsiveness of modern software. The viewer should sense care in material boundaries, movement, typography, and interactions—not in visible logos or gratuitous gold.

**Product proposition:** *One player. Five craftsmanship traditions. A single motion to change the object you watch through.*

**Why this has merit:** Instead of a flat CSS theme change, each variant owns a distinct frame, control metaphor, material language, and micro-interaction. They all express the same coherent ÉCRIN brand.

## Design principles (ranked)

1. **The video is the precious object.** Maximize picture size. Decorative casing is never more visually important than footage.
2. **Material plausibility.** Leather belongs at an edge or grip, brass at a functional junction, glass over a screen—not random texture everywhere.
3. **Functional ornament.** A zipper, clasp, buckle or crown should convey a real state and be manipulable in a familiar way.
4. **Restraint.** One hero detail per variant; all other elements should quietly support it.
5. **Consistency within diversity.** Controls can look different but use stable icon meanings, time formatting, positioning principles, input behavior and accessibility.
6. **Timeless, not gaudy.** Avoid excessive embossing, generic gold gradients, noisy particle effects, forced motion, faux hardware in impossible places, or heavy decorative headers.
7. **Multi-size excellence.** A smaller embed must be intentional, not a scaled-down desktop screenshot.
8. **No luxury-house impersonation.** No direct marks, names, monograms or signature motifs of Louis Vuitton, Hermès, Cartier, Rolex, etc.

## Three distances of perception

- **Far glance:** recognizable video area, main play action, timeline, distinct variant silhouette.
- **Normal use:** legible timestamps, mute state, clear play/pause, settings and fullscreen.
- **Close inspection:** fine leather grain, stitching intervals, edge painting, knurling, brushed-metal anisotropy, inset seams, optical alignment, physical microstates.

The third distance creates delight; the first two govern usability.

## Mood vocabulary

Tailored; quietly tactile; precise; relaxed; inherited but modern; sensory; edited; bespoke; enduring. **Not:** flamboyant, baroque, overly ornate, expensive-looking for its own sake, casino-gold, game HUD.

## How this differs from KITBASH

Kitbash is intentionally modular and playful: users mix components in a workshop. **ÉCRIN is curated and composed**: users select one of five complete, art-directed luxury objects from an in-player appearance menu. There is **no per-component Frankenstein editor** in scope. This is a separate concept and separate name.

## Shared storytelling

A restrained reveal: a neutral player appears in a considered frame. The viewer opens Settings → Appearance → Skin and switches from brown luggage leather to equestrian harness, watchmaker precision, folio simplicity, and grand-tourer cockpit. Media stays uninterrupted. The drama comes from a smooth object transformation without breaking playback.

## Creative review rubric

Evaluate each candidate mockup/implementation from 1–5 for: recognizable silhouette, originality of signature interaction, believable material, typography, contrast, low visual noise, mobile adaptation, and ability to understand playback in two seconds. A pretty concept that fails any essential control is rejected.
