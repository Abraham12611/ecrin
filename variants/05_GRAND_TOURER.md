# Variant 05 — GRAND TOURER / The Touring Edition

**ID:** `grand-tourer` · **Signature interaction:** amber precision rail + cockpit volume dial · **Reference:** [Grand Tourer concept](../references/05_grand_tourer_mockup.png)

## One-line identity

The poised cabin of a refined touring car: tailored leather, dark wood or lacquer, satin nickel and controlled pools of warm amber light—more grand tourer than race car.

## Distinction / silhouette

A smoothly sculpted panoramic frame with elegantly rounded shoulders and a purposeful central control console. It is the only variant permitted a shallow upper-center dial motif; do not take useful space from the video. Darker and more cinematic than the others, with metal used as a structural accent rather than brass jewelry.

## Materials and palette

Onyx leather (`#181716`), warm walnut veneer, dark nickel (`#8F8578`), subtle cognac seam, tiny amber light `#EA9C50`. Absolutely no automobile logos, gauges directly copied from existing cars, or dashboard stereotypes such as huge speedometers.

## Controls

- **Play/pause:** single confident circular transport button like a finely weighted push switch, with white play/pause icon. Warm ring indicates focus/active, never the only cue.
- **Seek:** a long **backlit progress rail**, with a small machined thumb; played segment glows amber, unplayed dark grey. Hover/focus timestamp appears in a calm tool-tip.
- **Volume:** tactile knurled rotary knob with bounded arc and a nearby distinct mute icon; number revealed on interaction.
- **Time:** elegant tabular dashboard numerals, large enough and stable.
- **Settings:** console-inspired compact drawer; keep Appearance visible inside normal settings hierarchy.
- **Captions/fullscreen:** minimal engraved toggles with strong contrast against dark chrome.

## Motion and sound

Restrained illumination responses (<200 ms), no engine-start video sound, simulated acceleration, or continuous moving light bars. Avoid making device look like a racing-gaming HUD. User should see the video first.

## States

Paused: illuminated border settles. Playing: progress advances with clear fill. Muted: knob still retains volume but mute button explicitly changes. Buffering: small honest loading indicator; don't animate the progress track beyond actual buffered/playback state. Disabled/unsupported: no ghost dials.

## Compact form

Upper-center gauge disappears; metal highlights simplify; volume dial becomes a popover slider if required; seek remains full width. Icon and text contrast survive light footage. Resize must not make the outer console taller than necessary.

## Must-have deliverables

- Inset frame, control strip, rotary hardware, backlit seek track assets.
- Desktop, narrow, settings, focus, dark-footage/bright-footage views.
- Smooth but low-cost illumination CSS and reduced-motion equivalent.

## Rejection conditions

Unnecessary dashboard gauges, car logos, illegible glowing type, excessive animated LEDs, huge 3D dial consuming seek area, reliance on shader-heavy effects.
