# Variant 04 — FOLIO / The Leather Edition

**ID:** `folio` · **Signature interaction:** fold-out settings panel · **Reference:** [Folio concept](../references/04_folio_mockup.png)

## One-line identity

The most minimal expression of ÉCRIN: the understated tactility of an exceptionally made leather folio, lined with felt and finished with quiet stitching rather than showpiece metal.

## Distinction / silhouette

Flat, soft rectangular composition, fine leather radius and clean inner border; one discreet fold at the top or edge, spacious control footer. No protruding corner caps, no large buckles, no watch crest. Let video breathe. The reference image is a useful starting tone, but production should reduce unnecessary border thickness.

## Materials/palette

Walnut (`#69422F`) or deep dark brown, linen (`#E9DFCE`), soft suede interior, restrained warm brass. Low-contrast leather grain only on structural edges; time and icons sit on a solid opaque strip.

## Controls

- **Play/pause:** small leather-wrapped square press button with a legible white/ivory triangle/pause symbol; no pseudo-embossed decorative lettering.
- **Seek:** thin **stitched ribbon rail** with a brass or ivory keeper thumb. Stroke-style progress is distinct from an ornamental seam. Continuous accurate seeking.
- **Volume:** understated conventional horizontal slider plus a compact embossed mute; Folio is the deliberately *less gimmicky* variant.
- **Time:** discreet but legible tabular numerals; do not shrink below practical readability.
- **Settings:** primary expressive move—touch gear and a small leather-lined insert **unfolds** from the right edge, like a folio flap. Despite visual illusion, it is a true settings popover with normal navigation and keyboard focus.
- **Captions/fullscreen:** minimal chrome, fine ivory line icons, no faux-gold decor.

## Interaction discipline

The fold should not physically push the video offscreen or block essential playback controls. In a large player, panel rises/opens above controls. On a narrow player, it appears as an accessible inset sheet with no elaborate page-turn transition. The apparent hinge is decorative; menu behavior must follow normal disclosure/dialog controls.

## State system

Playing/paused icon crisp; focus accent obvious against dark leather; timeline progress and buffered sections distinct; menu opening respects reduced motion; errors appear on clean opaque overlay with readable message.

## Compact form

This variant should be the **best-performing small-screen baseline** for all others. Nearly flat leather outline, two short control rows if needed, straightforward slider UI and full text readability. A narrow embed should feel like fine stationery, not a miniature handbag.

## Must-have deliverables

- Minimal texture tiles, ribbon seek thumb, folded menu backing assets.
- Desktop and mobile base states, Appearance menu opened, keyboard-focused controls.
- Side-by-side comparison with neutral Video.js skin to prove no loss of function.

## Rejection conditions

Over-decoration, hidden controls, page-turn covering captions, low-contrast leather-on-leather icons, oversized brand plaque, unreadably subtle progress bar.
