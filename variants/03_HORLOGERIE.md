# Variant 03 — HORLOGERIE / The Watchmaker Edition

**ID:** `horlogerie` · **Signature interaction:** precision crown volume · **Reference:** [Watchmaker concept](../references/03_horlogerie_watchmaker_mockup.png)

## One-line identity

The precision and quiet ceremony of horology, translated into a video interface: calibrated time, milled surfaces, a tactile crown, jewel-like markers and optical discipline.

## Distinction / silhouette

The only fully metal-forward variant. A slim satin-champagne frame with a precise lower instrument panel, thin burgundy enamel inlays, shallow decorative screws, and a single small ruby-like marker. **No watch face covering the video.** Unlike Voyage/Bridle, the player should feel engineered rather than stitched.

## Palette/material

Champagne metal (`#CBB28A`), deep bordeaux enamel (`#4F172C`), subtle ruby (`#9E3150`), dark inner glass, very restrained polished edge highlights. Micro-engraving belongs only to decoration; functional labels must remain generously sized.

## Controls

- **Play/pause:** large enough round pusher with a triangle/pause icon; short inward travel.
- **Seek:** a ruler-like linear measurement rail with refined engraved time ticks, distinct fill, and jewel-style thumb. Use tabular numerals and actual time values; never make seek a watch hand if it harms accuracy.
- **Volume:** **milled winding-crown dial** with circular range indication. Rotating/dragging adjusts volume within 0–100%, without endless-spin confusion; if pointer circular logic proves inaccessible, keep crown visuals but use a semantic linear range or accessible popup to operate it.
- **Mute:** separate visible action, ideally a small raised indicator/speaker icon.
- **Settings:** minimal movement of a precision compartment. Use a simple elegant panel with well-spaced options; functional UI stays flat enough to read.
- **Captions/fullscreen:** dark enamel icon buttons; clear contrast and focus treatment.

## Motion

No ticking sound, moving hands, or continuous oscillations by default. Crown turns immediately with volume level, progress jewel moves with elapsed time, settings panel arrives with a measured 160–200 ms animation. Respect reduced-motion preference.

## State articulation

- Focus: high-visibility outline contrasting against champagne metal.
- Active seek: subtle engraved scale highlight; numerical tooltip.
- Muted: explicit icon and state text; don't rely on knob orientation alone.
- Buffering: small understated indicator not mistaken for a chronograph sweep.
- Captions open: panel must not overlap the volume crown awkwardly.

## Compact form

The crown may compress into a tappable volume button that opens a full-size slider. Collapse decorative upper jewel crest and screws. Seek rail uses fewer ornamental ticks but remains continuous. Ensure fixed-size time digits do not shift.

## Must-have deliverables

- Metal/engraving asset kit, active/focus/pressed crown, time rail, numerical typography study.
- Desktop, compact, settings and volume-interaction frames.
- Circular volume mapping tests, plus keyboard and mobile fallback.

## Rejection conditions

Cluttered clock gauges, impossible tiny controls, faux Rolex/Cartier marks, an excessively elaborate radial menu, low-contrast numerals, visible time display jumping due to proportional digits.
